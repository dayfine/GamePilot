# Shining Pearl battle audit

This audit covers the first-clear route in [Shining Pearl Any% Glitchless speedrun](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fgI5MyC1jGI). It records **99 visible encounters** from the Lake Verity starter fight through Cynthia. The [route transcript](bdsp-speedrun-video-notes.md#coverage) covers all 40 playback blocks. Battle windows include the nearby lead-in and victory text where the boundaries are approximate.

- **94 defeat encounters** comprise 93 trainer battles and the opening wild Starly. One row represents one battle screen, including linked trainer doubles. The remaining five rows cover two escapes, two captures and Dawn’s tutorial.
- **A · attack selection (63)** covers damage selection, priority, incidental attack effects, move locks and forced replacements. **B · recovery (1)** adds a deliberate in-battle heal. **C · setup and coordination (30)** adds a planned non-damaging move, X item, voluntary switch or double-battle target and partner coordination. The tier describes the route’s demonstrated actions. Minimum sufficient policy depends on the required reliability. [Pokeguy action tiers](https://github.com/dayfine/PokemonSimulation/blob/main/docs/battles/pokeguy-action-complexity.md)
- The [entry stat sheet](https://github.com/dayfine/PokemonSimulation/blob/7270b6b03f2f5e21b072b4bdbab28afceb693ed7/data/runs/bdsp-speedrun/bdsp-speedrun-entry-stats.md) gives each party member’s species, level, HP, held item, moves and numerical stat inputs. The [fixture ledger](https://github.com/dayfine/PokemonSimulation/blob/7270b6b03f2f5e21b072b4bdbab28afceb693ed7/data/runs/bdsp-speedrun/bdsp-speedrun-fixtures.json) includes opposing and allied trainer sets with nature, EVs, IVs and ability. The stat sheet labels HP reconstructed from progression. Fainted Pokémon stay in the recorded party order.
- **R / D** lists two independently seeded, per-battle win estimates. **R** uses recorded opening moves where transcribed, then variable move selection. **D** favors high expected damage. Both use a route-informed player policy and resample damage, critical hits, accuracy and eligible effects. Each battle starts fresh from its entry state. The [model and its limitations](#model-and-reproduction) describe the conditions behind each number.

## Model and reproduction

The model uses Pokémon Showdown’s BDSP battle mechanics and the datamined remake sets. The player follows recorded move and item sequences where encoded, falling back to damage-scored move choice when a planned action becomes unavailable. The tactical rival and boss policy scores damaging moves and selected status moves. The Platinum decomp provides a reference for singles and doubles scoring, random ties and low-HP medicine. Opponent choice weights, healing stocks and replacement order in the trials are approximations. The doubles model combines each side into one Showdown team and approximates partner choices and replacement ownership. [Showdown BDSP mechanics](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/tree/master/data/mods/gen8bdsp) · [Platinum trainer AI](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/485c400f9d7bad10d906d556ba23e5e46d5b3e5e/src/battle/trainer_ai/trainer_ai.c)

Known HP overrides the calculated maximum. Where current HP is missing, R uses full HP and D uses 75% of maximum. Both use reconstructed EV totals, point assumptions for unknown player IVs and natures, and base PP where the footage provides no tracked value. Selected midfight level-ups are implemented. The model omits affection bonuses and the cartridge RNG trace. Early survival can depend on an Ember burn, speed tie or damage roll. Late setup can depend more on the opponent’s exact choice than on random damage. R and D are scenario comparisons. The [raw counts](https://github.com/dayfine/PokemonSimulation/blob/7270b6b03f2f5e21b072b4bdbab28afceb693ed7/data/runs/bdsp-speedrun/bdsp-speedrun-results.json) retain per-scenario wins, losses and unresolved trials. Multiplying the per-battle rates would assume an untested distribution of entry states. [BDSP trade and encounter constants](https://github.com/kwsch/PKHeX/blob/a5311f9f55a84376fd2e2499b25d9fb9640d9f2d/PKHeX.Core/Legality/Encounters/Data/Gen8/Encounters8b.cs) · [Species EV yields](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/blob/master/data/v2/csv/pokemon_stats.csv)

Each rate uses 256 trials in each scenario. Displayed rates round to whole percentages. A displayed 0% or 100% reflects the sampled outcomes under these inputs. Values within half a percentage point of either endpoint are shown as <1% or >99% when both outcomes occurred. Machop’s one-ball capture range uses its green HP band, an ordinary Poké Ball, no status and Lv. 9 Chimchar versus Lv. 7 Machop. Palkia’s capture is guaranteed conditional on spending the Master Ball. The two Geodude entries have no modeled escape rate because foe Speed and the exact escape inputs are unavailable. [BDSP capture formula implementation](https://github.com/thames1000/pokemon-catch-rate-calculator/blob/master/desktop/catch_formula.py) · [Platinum Machop species data](https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/blob/485c400f9d7bad10d906d556ba23e5e46d5b3e5e/res/pokemon/machop/data.json)

## Fights below 60% in every tested scenario

Two fights stayed below 60% across all available baseline and follow-up runs. The table uses the highest **unrounded** estimate for each fight. It ranks the tested policies and entry states. The remaining work is to test the controller changes described below.

| Fight | Best tested rate | Main failure point |
|---|---:|---|
| Crasher Wake | 46.9% | Quagsire can disrupt Kazza’s Speed or setup. The switch and X Speed sequence depends on when Monferno faints. |
| Candice | 48.4% | Snover’s Avalanche and hail drain the solo lead’s HP before faster Sneasel can attack. |

**Crasher Wake.** Gyarados takes 4× from Shock Wave. Quagsire’s Ground type blocks Shock Wave and its typing takes 4× from Grass Knot. Quagsire can lower Kazza’s Speed with Mud Shot, remove boosts with Haze, or set rain for Floatzel’s Swift Swim. Floatzel takes 2× from Grass Knot and has 85 modeled Speed to Kazza’s 70 before boosts. Aqua Jet can act first even after X Speed. The recording uses Switch mode to send Monferno in as Quagsire enters. Monferno uses Taunt and faints at 48/80 HP to Mud Shot, allowing Kazza to return for free and use X Speed. At 80/80 HP, Monferno can survive that hit and receive the scripted X Speed itself. A revised policy should verify that Kazza is active before boosting and account for any turn spent switching back. [Entry stats](https://github.com/dayfine/PokemonSimulation/blob/7270b6b03f2f5e21b072b4bdbab28afceb693ed7/data/runs/bdsp-speedrun/bdsp-speedrun-entry-stats.md#battle-42) · [Recorded turns](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fgI5MyC1jGI&t=4942s)

**Candice.** Kazza already enters at 107/107 HP. In the recording, it uses X Sp. Atk while Snover’s Avalanche and hail take it to 49/107. Snover’s Icy Rock extends the hail, and Sneasel has 124 modeled Speed to Kazza’s 108. If the first exchange leaves Kazza too low, it may fall before it can land the super-effective Dazzling Gleam on Sneasel. Medicham’s Pure Power and Abomasnow’s ability to restart hail and set Aurora Veil add pressure if the sweep slips. Test recovery or Speed setup against Snover with enough HP left to take the next attack and hail tick. [Entry stats](https://github.com/dayfine/PokemonSimulation/blob/7270b6b03f2f5e21b072b4bdbab28afceb693ed7/data/runs/bdsp-speedrun/bdsp-speedrun-entry-stats.md#battle-62) · [Recorded turns](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fgI5MyC1jGI&t=7465s)

The Route 203 rival’s reactive Potion tests won every sampled trial. Flint was just outside the cutoff at 60.9% in one matched-policy scenario.
