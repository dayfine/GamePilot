# Team Training (FRLG)

Registry id `frlg.team_training`, group FRLG / Training (`src/program_registry`). It turns the Mail
Glitch's item duplication into levels (roadmap A1, issue #10): Rare Candies are copied, then fed to the
members a goal names. It needs a save where `frlg.mail_glitch` has run to mg-06 (MIMIEN in slot 1
with the glitched mail record, mail on slots 2-6). It runs on mGBA and on the Switch.

| Checkpoint | Content |
|---|---|
| tt-01 | Route 6's hidden Rare Candy (a Super Repel first), 98 duplication rounds: 99 candies |

The leveling checkpoints follow once the `candy_level` action lands: Blastoise to Lv 100 as the lead,
then the dex-collection catchers (Parasect with Spore, Haunter with Mean Look and Hypnosis).
